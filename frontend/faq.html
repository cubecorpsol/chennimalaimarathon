Frequently Asked Questions

Get all the essential information about the Chennimalai Marathon.

When and where is the Chennimalai Marathon held?

The Chennimalai Marathon is held annually in Chennimalai, Tamil Nadu, with flag-off at 5:55 AM. The exact date and venue details will be announced on our website and official communication channels. Please stay tuned for updates!

What are the race categories and distances?

We offer a 3.5KM Fun Run for participants aged 13 years and below, and a 7KM Timed Run for participants aged above 13 years.

Who can participate in the marathon?

Anyone who meets the event's age eligibility can participate. Children aged 5–13 years can join the 3.5 KM run, while participants aged above 13 years can take part in the 7 KM run. Whether you're a beginner, fitness enthusiast, or experienced runner, you're welcome to join and enjoy the marathon.

How do I register for the event?

You can register for the event by clicking the "Register Now" button on our website and completing the registration form.

What is the registration fee for the marathon?

Early Bird (Until August 15): ₹100 for Kids (Below 13 years), ₹150 for Others (Above 13 years).
Regular Registration (August 16–20): ₹200 for all registrations.
Registrations close after August 20.
The Official Chennimalai Marathon T-Shirt is complimentary and included automatically for runners aged 13+ (7 KM category).

Will I receive a T-shirt and finisher medal?

All registered participants receive a finisher medal upon completing the race. The Official Chennimalai Marathon T-Shirt is complimentary and included automatically for runners aged 13+ (7 KM category).

Is medical support available during the run?

Yes. Participant safety is our highest priority. The following medical support will be available throughout the marathon route and at the finish area: Qualified Doctors, Certified Registered Nurses (CRN), Anesthesia Support Team, Physiotherapy Team, Cardiology Emergency Support, Ambulance Services, and First Aid Stations. Medical volunteers will be available throughout the event for immediate assistance.

Where is the starting and finishing location?

The Chennimalai Marathon will start and finish at Kongu Vellalar Matriculation Higher Secondary School, Chennimalai. Both the 7 KM Timed Run and the 3.5 KM Fun Run (Kids Category) share the same starting and finishing point. The exact reporting instructions and route map will be shared with all registered participants before the event.

Is car parking available?

Yes. Dedicated parking facilities will be available near the event venue. Our volunteers will guide participants to the designated parking areas for hassle-free parking.

Will transportation be provided after completing the marathon?

Yes. Transportation facilities will be arranged to bring participants back to the original starting location after completing the marathon. Volunteers will assist participants in boarding the return vehicles.

When will my T-shirt be confirmed?

T-shirt confirmation will be sent to all eligible registered participants through their registered email address on 21st August. Participants are requested to check both Inbox and Spam folders.

When and where can I collect my Marathon Kit and T-shirt?

The Marathon Kit collection date, venue and timing will be communicated through the registered email approximately 2 days before the event. Participants must carry their Registration Confirmation and a valid ID proof while collecting the kit.

Are facilities available for children?

Yes. Basic facilities and volunteer support will be available for children participating in the Kids Run. Parents or guardians are requested to accompany young participants throughout the event.

What time should participants report?

Participants are requested to report at least 60 minutes before their scheduled race start time. This provides sufficient time for parking, check-in, warm-up and announcements.

Is baggage deposit available?

Yes. A supervised baggage deposit counter will be available at the venue. Participants are advised not to leave valuables inside the baggage counter.

Will drinking water and refreshments be provided?

Yes. Hydration points will be available throughout the marathon route. Refreshments and snacks will be provided after successfully completing the marathon.

Can I transfer my registration to another participant?

No. Registrations are strictly non-transferable after confirmation.

How will I receive important event updates?

All important announcements including T-shirt Confirmation, Kit Collection Details, Reporting Instructions, Parking Information, Event Guidelines, and Last Minute Announcements will be sent to your registered email address. Participants are advised to check their email regularly before the event.

How can my company sponsor the Chennimalai Marathon?

Sponsorship sign-ups for the current edition are now closed. For sponsorship enquiries, please reach out to us via our Contact page and our team will get back to you.

Are sponsorship payments refundable?

Sponsorship contributions are non-refundable once event branding assets, banners, t-shirts, or promotional materials have been sent for printing and production. For sponsorship queries, contact info@chennimalaimarathon.com. See our Refund Policy for full details.

Whom can I contact for more details?

For any further queries, please reach out to us through our Contact page and we will be happy to assist you.
