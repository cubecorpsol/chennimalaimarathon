PRIVACY POLICY

Learn how we protect, manage, and utilize your personal information.

Authorized Merchant & Event Manager

The official Chennimalai Marathon portal and online registration payment processing are managed and operated by Zeblio (managed by SNS Emporium).

Registered Address: 199/1, Pasumai Nagar, Periya Chettipalayam, Pudur, Erode - 638002, Tamil Nadu.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for Chennimalai Marathon 2026 or submit an event sponsorship application, we collect personal and organizational information necessary to process your request and ensure event administration. This includes:

  • Personal Details: Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Blood Group, and T-shirt size (for marathon registration).
  • Contact Information: Mobile Phone Number, Email Address, District, and Pincode.
  • Emergency Support Data: Emergency Contact Number (for marathon participants).
  • Sponsorship Details: Company / brand name, contact person, designation, GSTIN (optional), website, sponsorship tier, contribution amount, and any notes provided on the sponsorship form.
  • Transaction Metadata: Payment Gateway Order IDs, Transaction Reference IDs, and payment verification signatures (we do not store credit/debit card numbers, CVV, or net banking passwords).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal and organizational data is used strictly for event administration, sponsor recognition, and related communications, specifically to:

  • Verify participant age and assign appropriate race categories (3.5 KM Fun Run vs. 7 KM Timed Run).
  • Generate official T-shirt numbers and digital runner Bibs.
  • Send automated registration / sponsorship confirmation emails, payment receipts, and event-day instructions.
  • Display approved sponsor names, logos, and websites on the public sponsors wall (home, prizes, and sponsors pages) as part of event recognition.
  • Provide critical emergency medical support on race day using your disclosed blood group and emergency contact.

3. Payment Processing & Gateway Security

All online payment transactions are processed through certified PCI-DSS compliant and RBI-approved online payment gateways under our merchant entity, Zeblio (managed by SNS Emporium), which manages and operates the official Chennimalai Marathon portal. At no point do Chennimalai Marathon servers capture or store sensitive banking or card credentials. Payment gateways handle payment authorization over secure, 256-bit SSL encrypted connections.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared only with essential service partners directly involved in conducting the marathon:

  • Payment Gateway Providers: For secure transaction authorization and merchant banking compliance.
  • Timing & Bib Vendors: For printing runner bibs and recording official race finishes.
  • Medical Teams: In the event of a health emergency during the marathon.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use basic browser session storage and temporary cookies to facilitate smooth navigation between registration steps and ensure payment window stability. You can modify your browser settings to decline cookies, though this may impact registration form functionality.

6. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption and secure Google Cloud / Sheets database integration, to safeguard your registration records against unauthorized access. Participant records are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill event reporting, auditing, and legal compliance.

7. Contact for Privacy Concerns

If you have any questions regarding our Privacy Policy or wish to request correction or deletion of your data after the event, please contact our data protection team at info@chennimalaimarathon.com.
