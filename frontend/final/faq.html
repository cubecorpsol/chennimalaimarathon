Frequently Asked Questions

Get all the essential information about the Chennimalai Marathon.

When and where is the Chennimalai Marathon held?

The Chennimalai Marathon is held annually in Chennimalai, Tamil Nadu, with flag-off at 5:30 AM. The exact date and venue details will be announced on our website and official communication channels. Please stay tuned for updates!

What are the race categories and distances?

We offer a 5KM Fun Run for participants below 13 years, and a 10KM Timed Run for participants above 13 years.

Who can participate in the marathon?

Runners of all ages and fitness levels are welcome to participate, from professionals and athletes to students and fitness enthusiasts.

How do I register for the event?

You can register for the event by clicking the "Register Now" button on our website and completing the registration form.

Will I receive a T-shirt and finisher medal?

Yes, all registered participants will receive an event T-shirt and a finisher medal upon completing the race.

Is medical support available during the run?

Yes, medical support and first-aid teams will be stationed along the route and at the finish line for the safety of all runners.

Whom can I contact for more details?

For any further queries, please reach out to us through our Contact page and we will be happy to assist you.
